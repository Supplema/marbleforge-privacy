# Marble Forge: The Forge Trials — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-24_

**Marble Forge does not collect, store, or share any personal data.**

- The game has **no accounts**, **no network features**, **no ads**,
  and **no third-party SDKs**.
- All progress and gameplay statistics are stored **only on your
  device**, and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
- The game requests **no permissions** beyond what Android grants
  every app by default.
- Nothing you do in the game ever leaves your phone.

If a future update adds any online feature, this policy will be
updated before that feature ships, and the store listing's Data Safety
section will reflect it.
