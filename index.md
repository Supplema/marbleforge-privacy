# Marble Forge: The Forge Trials — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-06_

**Marble Forge has no accounts, no ads, no third-party SDKs, and asks
for nothing personal. It never collects your name, email, contacts,
location, or any advertising or device identifier.**

## Playing on your own

Practice, Daily Trial, Rival Trial and pass-and-play are **entirely
offline**. Progress and statistics are stored **only on your device**
and are deleted when you uninstall the app. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## Playing a duel with a friend

Online duels are the one feature that uses the internet, and only when
you start or join one.

- When you duel, the app sends **the moves of that duel** — which
  column you launched, which powers you used — to a relay server
  (Google Firebase Realtime Database) so your rival's phone can replay
  them. This is what makes the duel work.
- Each duel is stored under a **random duel id**. That id is the only
  key. It is not linked to you, your phone, or any account, because
  none of those exist.
- The transfer is **encrypted in transit** (HTTPS only).
- Duel histories are kept so a duel can be resumed. They contain
  **game moves and nothing else**.

## What we do not do

- No accounts, sign-in, or single sign-on
- No advertising, tracking, or profiling
- No analytics sent off your device — the game's own statistics stay
  in a local file on your phone
- No sale or sharing of data with other companies

## Data deletion

Because there are no accounts, there is **no way to identify which
duels belong to which person** — and so no deletion request process.
Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. Uninstalling
also ends your access to any duel in progress.

## Permissions

The app requests **internet access** (for online duels) and
**vibration** (for haptic feedback). Nothing else.

## Children

Marble Forge is a puzzle game suitable for general audiences. It
collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a future update changes what leaves your device, this policy will be
updated before that feature ships, and the store listing's Data Safety
section will reflect it.
